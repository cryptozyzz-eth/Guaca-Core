# Demo photos, Medellín

Every photograph in this directory is a freely licensed Wikimedia Commons
image, downloaded once (2026-10-19) as a 960 px thumbnail and stripped of
metadata. Credits and licences are also carried per place in the seeded
`public_profile.image` record. Keep this manifest in sync when adding files.

No photo here is evidence of a local visit: it describes a place, nothing
more. Parque Lleras and Mercado del Río have no freely-licensed photograph
that clearly shows them, so their profiles ship without one.
